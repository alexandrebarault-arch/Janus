## 2. Why componentwise conservation is not yet the Aoki scalar equation

After Aoki's constraint reduction the two coordinate Stückelberg perturbations are not independent. They are generated by one scalar `pi`:

`delta_eta = A pi_eta + B pi_r`,

`delta_r   = D pi_eta + C pi_r`.

Therefore, if the coordinate residuals are denoted `(E_eta,E_r)`, the one-field Euler residual is the adjoint of this map,

`E_pi = -partial_eta(A E_eta + D E_r) - partial_r(B E_eta + C E_r)`.

Testing `E_eta=0` and `E_r=0` separately after the one-field reduction is too strong and can reject a configuration that satisfies the actual reduced scalar equation.
