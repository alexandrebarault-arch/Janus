# Decision Log

## 2026-10-11 — Research protocol

- Treat Janus as a falsifiable research program, not as a doctrine to defend.
- Separate mathematical validity, physical interpretation, and empirical validation.
- Preserve rejected branches and corrections.

## 2026-10-11 — Published Janus not adopted as the working fundamental theory

Reason:

- constrained-variation issue in the 2015 derivation;
- 2024 interaction sector not fully specified for a closed nonlinear theory;
- exact equal-magnitude `+G/-G` interaction matrix is structurally singular in a two-sector canonical description.

Decision: keep `JANUS-EXACT` as an audit/control branch only.

## 2026-10-11 — Adopt ghost-free bimetric continuation

Decision: use a Hassan-Rosen-type ghost-free bimetric action as the mathematical skeleton for `JANUS-R`.

Motivation:

- explicit nonlinear interaction potential;
- two metric sectors;
- known removal of the Boulware-Deser ghost in the intended construction;
- scale-dependent mixing between massless and massive spin-2 modes can generate cross-sector effects without fundamental negative kinetic energy.

## 2026-10-11 — Do not require fundamental negative mass

Decision: both matter sectors remain positive-energy by default.

Reason: effective repulsion does not logically require a negative Hamiltonian sector. Negative fundamental energy will not be reintroduced unless forced by a derivation or evidence.

## 2026-10-11 — Reject minimal beta1 model as final candidate

Reason: acceptable/self-accelerating FLRW background is insufficient because the perturbation sector is unstable in the relevant early-time regime.

## 2026-10-11 — Retract beta1-beta4 infinite branch as main candidate

Earlier assessment over-weighted gradient stability. Subsequent check of Higuchi/healthy-branch conditions makes it unsuitable as the default research branch.

## 2026-10-11 — Reject constant-mass all-in-one twin-sector unification

Decision: the main branch will retain conventional CDM in the visible sector.

Reason: demanding one constant-mass twin sector to be both ordinary attractive dark matter and large-scale Janus-like repulsive matter requires incompatible interaction regimes across astrophysical scales.

## 2026-10-11 — Reject naive large-Compton-wavelength extrapolation

Earlier idea: choose `m_eff ~ 10^-32 eV` so the linear cross-sector repulsive regime extends to cosmic-web scales.

Correction: the same unscreened point-source solution is locally constrained. A viable cosmic-scale model requires nonlinear screening and cannot be obtained by extending the linear Yukawa solution to all radii.

## 2026-10-11 — Main branch is now JANUS-R Late + screening

The working hypothesis is deliberately narrower:

- GR-like behavior in screened local/high-density regions;
- possible late-time large-scale cross-sector effect in low-density/cosmic-web environments;
- test first around large voids;
- do not claim negative gravitational lensing before explicitly deriving the Weyl potential.
